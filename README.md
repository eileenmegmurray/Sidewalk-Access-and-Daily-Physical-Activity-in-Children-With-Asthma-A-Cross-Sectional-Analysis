### Sidewalk-Access-and-Daily-Physical-Activity-in-Children-With-Asthma-A-Cross-Sectional-Analysis

## Background 
Due to my interest in the urban environment, and children's health, I decided for my MPH capstone project, I wanted to explore something related to these topics. After a careful review of the literature, I had come to learn that children with asthma are much less likely to partake in physical activity and are at a higher risk of developing obesity than their peers who do not have asthma. Furthermore, I had learned in some studies with physical activity amongst children urban design features such as sidewalks. 

Thus to this end, I decided to utilize the National Survey on Children's Health (2023) tryto assess the association between parent-reported neighborhood sidewalk presence and daily physical activity among children with current asthma, and to examine whether neighborhood safety and neighborhood physical disorder modified this association.
